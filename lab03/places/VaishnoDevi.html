Vaishno Devi Temple

Katra, Jammu & Kashmir

About Vaishno Devi Temple

Vaishno Devi Temple is one of the holiest Hindu pilgrimage sites in India. Dedicated to Goddess Vaishno Devi, the temple is located in the Trikuta Hills and attracts millions of devotees every year.

Location

Katra, Reasi District, Jammu & Kashmir.

Significance

Dedicated to Goddess Vaishno Devi, the temple is considered one of the most sacred Shakti Peeth pilgrimage destinations in India.

Famous For

Holy cave shrine, Vaishno Devi Yatra, Trikuta Hills, and millions of annual pilgrims.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra Railway Station.
  • Airport: Jammu Airport (about 50 km).
  • Trek: 13 km trek from Katra to the temple.

Best Time to Visit

March to October and during Navratri festival.

Temple Timings

Open throughout the year, 24 hours for devotees.

Entry Fee

Free entry. Yatra registration is required before the pilgrimage.

Nearby Attractions

Bhairavnath Temple, Ardhkuwari Cave, Banganga, and Charan Paduka.
