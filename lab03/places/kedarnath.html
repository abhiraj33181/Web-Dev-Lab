Kedarnath Temple

Rudraprayag, Uttarakhand

About Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath Temple is one of the holiest temples dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is located in the Himalayan mountains and is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas.

Location

Rudraprayag District, Uttarakhand.

Significance

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, Kedarnath is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites and an important Jyotirlinga temple.

Famous For

Ancient Himalayan temple, Char Dham Yatra, and beautiful snow-covered surroundings.

How to Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (about 240 km).
  • Nearest Railway Station: Rishikesh Railway Station.
  • Trek: 16 km trek from Gaurikund to Kedarnath Temple.

Best Time to Visit

May to June and September to October.

Temple Timings

4:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Open only during the pilgrimage season).

Entry Fee

Free entry for devotees.

Nearby Attractions

Gaurikund, Bhairavnath Temple, Vasuki Tal, and Chorabari Tal.
