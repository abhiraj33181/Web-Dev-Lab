Jagannath Temple

Puri, Odisha

About Jagannath Temple

Jagannath Temple is one of the most famous Hindu temples in India. Dedicated to Lord Jagannath, it is one of the sacred Char Dham pilgrimage sites.

Location

Puri, Odisha.

Significance

Dedicated to Lord Jagannath, Lord Balabhadra, and Goddess Subhadra. It is one of the holiest pilgrimage destinations in India.

Famous For

Rath Yatra Festival, Char Dham pilgrimage, and the world's largest temple kitchen.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Puri Railway Station (about 3 km).
  • Airport: Biju Patnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar (about 60 km).
  • Road: Regular buses and taxis are available from Bhubaneswar and nearby cities.

Best Time to Visit

October to February and during the Rath Yatra festival.

Temple Timings

5:00 AM – 10:30 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free entry for devotees.

Nearby Attractions

Puri Beach, Gundicha Temple, Konark Sun Temple, and Chilika Lake.
