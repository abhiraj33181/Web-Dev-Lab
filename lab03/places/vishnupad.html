Vishnupad Temple

Gaya, Bihar

About Vishnupad Temple

Vishnupad Temple is one of the oldest and most sacred temples in Bihar. It is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and is an important pilgrimage site in Gaya.

Location

Gaya, Bihar (on the banks of the Falgu River).

Significance

The temple is believed to contain the sacred footprint of Lord Vishnu, making it one of the holiest Vaishnav pilgrimage sites.

Famous For

Lord Vishnu's footprint, Pind Daan rituals, and the annual Pitru Paksha Mela.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Gaya Junction (about 3 km).
  • Airport: Gaya International Airport (about 8 km).
  • Road: Easily reachable by bus, taxi, and auto from Gaya city.

Best Time to Visit

October to March.

Temple Timings

6:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free for all visitors.

Nearby Attractions

Falgu River, Mangla Gauri Temple, Brahmayoni Hills, and Mahabodhi Temple (Bodh Gaya).
