Mahakaleshwar Temple

Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

About Mahakaleshwar Temple

Mahakaleshwar Temple is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. It is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in India and is famous for its spiritual atmosphere.

Location

Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh.

Significance

Dedicated to Lord Shiva as Mahakal, the temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and is considered highly sacred.

Famous For

Bhasma Aarti, Jyotirlinga, and the grand celebration of Maha Shivratri.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Ujjain Junction (about 2 km).
  • Airport: Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport, Indore (about 55 km).
  • Road: Easily accessible by bus, taxi, and auto from Ujjain city.

Best Time to Visit

October to March.

Temple Timings

4:00 AM – 11:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free entry for general darshan. Special darshan tickets are available.

Nearby Attractions

Kal Bhairav Temple, Harsiddhi Temple, Ram Ghat, and Sandipani Ashram.
