Mahabodhi Temple

Bodh Gaya, Bihar

About Mahabodhi Temple

Mahabodhi Temple is one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the world. It is the place where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment under the sacred Bodhi Tree.

Location

Bodh Gaya, Gaya District, Bihar.

Significance

The temple marks the place where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Famous For

Sacred Bodhi Tree, Mahabodhi Temple, Buddhist meditation, and international pilgrims.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Gaya Junction (about 12 km).
  • Airport: Gaya International Airport (about 10 km).
  • Road: Well connected by buses, taxis, and auto-rickshaws from Gaya.

Best Time to Visit

October to March.

Temple Timings

5:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free entry for visitors.

Nearby Attractions

Great Buddha Statue, Bodhi Tree, Thai Monastery, Japanese Temple, and Sujata Stupa.
