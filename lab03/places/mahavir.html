Mahavir Mandir

Patna, Bihar

About Mahavir Mandir

Mahavir Mandir is one of the most famous temples dedicated to Lord Hanuman in India. Located near Patna Junction, it attracts millions of devotees every year.

Location

Patna, Bihar (Near Patna Junction Railway Station).

Significance

Dedicated to Lord Hanuman and believed to fulfill devotees' wishes and remove obstacles.

Famous For

Hanuman idol, Naivedyam Prasad, and special Tuesday & Saturday prayers.

How to Reach

  • Rail: Patna Junction (2-minute walk).
  • Air: Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport (7 km).
  • Road: Easily accessible by bus, taxi, and auto.

Best Time to Visit

October to March.

Temple Timings

5:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free for all visitors.

Nearby Attractions

Buddha Smriti Park, Golghar, Gandhi Maidan, and Patna Museum.
