Khatu Shyam Temple

Sikar, Rajasthan

About Khatu Shyam Temple

Khatu Shyam Temple is one of the most famous temples in Rajasthan. It is dedicated to Khatu Shyam Ji, who is believed to be Barbarika, the grandson of Bhima from the Mahabharata.

Location

Khatu Village, Sikar District, Rajasthan.

Significance

Dedicated to Khatu Shyam Ji, who is worshipped as an incarnation of Lord Krishna and is believed to fulfill devotees' wishes.

Famous For

Khatu Shyam Ji Darshan, Phalguna Mela, and devotional bhajans.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Ringas Junction (about 18 km).
  • Airport: Jaipur International Airport (about 95 km).
  • Road: Regular buses and taxis are available from Jaipur, Sikar, and Ringas.

Best Time to Visit

October to March and during the Phalguna Mela festival.

Temple Timings

5:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free entry for all devotees.

Nearby Attractions

Shyam Kund, Shyam Garden, Jeen Mata Temple, and Harshnath Temple.
