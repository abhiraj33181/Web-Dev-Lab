Shiv Khori Temple

Reasi, Jammu & Kashmir

About Shiv Khori Temple

Shiv Khori Temple is a famous cave shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva. The temple is located inside a natural cave and is an important pilgrimage destination in Jammu & Kashmir.

Location

Reasi District, Jammu & Kashmir.

Significance

The cave contains a naturally formed Shiva Lingam and is considered a sacred place for devotees of Lord Shiva.

Famous For

Natural Shiva Lingam, sacred cave, and Maha Shivratri pilgrimage.

How to Reach

  • Railway Station: Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (about 80 km).
  • Airport: Jammu Airport (about 100 km).
  • Road: Regular buses and taxis are available from Katra and Jammu.

Best Time to Visit

October to March and during Maha Shivratri.

Temple Timings

6:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Daily).

Entry Fee

Free for all visitors.

Nearby Attractions

Vaishno Devi Temple, Baba Dhansar, Nau Devi Cave, and Salal Dam.
