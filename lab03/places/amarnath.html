Amarnath Temple

Anantnag, Jammu & Kashmir

About Amarnath Temple

Amarnath Temple is one of the holiest shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is located inside a natural cave in the Himalayas and is famous for the naturally formed ice Shiva Lingam.

Location

Anantnag District, Jammu & Kashmir.

Significance

The temple is one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites of Lord Shiva and is believed to be the place where Shiva revealed the secret of immortality.

Famous For

Natural Ice Shiva Lingam, Amarnath Yatra, and the sacred Himalayan cave.

How to Reach

  • Airport: Srinagar International Airport (about 90 km).
  • Railway Station: Jammu Tawi Railway Station.
  • Road: Reach Pahalgam or Baltal by road, then trek to the cave.

Best Time to Visit

July to August during the Amarnath Yatra.

Temple Timings

Open only during the annual Amarnath Yatra season.

Entry Fee

Free entry. Yatra registration is required for pilgrims.

Nearby Attractions

Pahalgam, Baltal, Sheshnag Lake, and Lidder Valley.
