Explore the Sacred Temples of India

Discover India's most famous temples, their history, culture, and travel information.

Vaishno Devi Temple

Location: Katra, Jammu & Kashmir

Significance: Major pilgrimage dedicated to Goddess Vaishno Devi.

Travel: Trek from Katra or use helicopter service.

Mahavir Mandir

Location: Patna, Bihar

Significance: FLord Hanuman idol, Naivedyam Prasad, and large crowds on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Travel: Best time to visit is October–March. Nearest railway station is Patna Junction, and the nearest airport is Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport, Patna.

Shri Vishnupad Temple

Location: Gaya, Bihar

Significance: A sacred temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, believed to mark the footprint (Vishnupad) of Lord Vishnu on a stone.

Travel: Best time to visit is October–March. Nearest railway station is Gaya Junction, and the nearest airport is Gaya International Airport (about 8 km).

Mahabodhi Temple

Location: Gaya, Bihar

Significance: One of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites, where Buddha is believed to have attained enlightenment.

Travel: Best time to visit is October–March. Nearest railway station is Gaya Junction, and the nearest airport is Gaya International Airport (about 12 km)

Mahakaleshwar Temple

Location: Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

Significance: Famous Jyotirlinga and Bhasma Aarti.

Travel: Best time: October–March.

Amarnath Temple

Location: Anantnag District, Jammu & Kashmir

Significance: Sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Travel: Best time: June–September.

Shiv Khori Temple

Location: Reasi District, Jammu & Kashmir

Significance: A sacred cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, believed to contain a naturally formed Shiva Lingam inside the cave.

Travel: Best time to visit is October–March and during Maha Shivratri. Nearest railway station is Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra, and the nearest airport is Jammu Airport.

Jagannath Temple

Location: Puri, Odisha

Significance: One of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites.

Travel: Famous for Rath Yatra festival.

Kedarnath Temple

Location: Uttarakhand

Significance: Sacred Himalayan temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Travel: Open May–October only.

Mahakaleshwar Temple

Location: Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

Significance: Famous Jyotirlinga and Bhasma Aarti.

Travel: Best time: October–March.
